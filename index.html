About me

I spent thirteen years keeping ships powered as a Navy Electrician's Mate, and I've spent the years since keeping networks observable as a Splunk engineer on federal programs. Both jobs taught me the same lesson: the systems that matter are the ones you can trust and explain. That is what pulled me toward artificial intelligence — not the hype, but the engineering problem of making machine decisions consistent, auditable, and understandable to the people who have to live with them.

At UAT I'm completing a dual B.S. in Artificial Intelligence and Advancing Computer Science. This site is my working record for the Student Innovation Project sequence: the SIP page holds everything I've built for FlagAI, the Boards page maps my coursework to the six program objectives I'll defend in SIP411, and the Projects page collects the rest of my in-major work.
